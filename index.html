Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

Your questions, answered.

Your questions, answered.

What services does your recruitment agency offer?

We provide a range of services, including permanent, and contract staffing, executive search, and specialized recruitment for various industries. We also offer workforce solutions like HR consulting, employee training, and onboarding support.

Can you help employers with hiring needs?

Absolutely! We partner with employers to understand their hiring needs and find the best candidates for their roles. Employers can contact us to discuss their recruitment needs, and we'll tailor our services to match their requirements.

What industries do you specialize in?

We specialize in a wide range of industries, including Customer Service, IT, finance, healthcare, engineering, retail, manufacturing, and more. We cater to both entry-level and executive roles.

How do you ensure confidentiality during the recruitment process?

We prioritize your privacy and confidentiality. Your personal information and application details are only shared with potential employers with your consent. We follow strict data protection policies to safeguard your information.

What happens after I’m placed in a job?

Our support doesn’t end once you’re placed. We check in with both you and the employer to ensure the placement is successful. If you have any issues or need further assistance, our team is here to help.

Is there a fee for job seekers to use your services?

No, our services are free for job seekers.
